1. siyi_capture_calibration.py  for capturing photos automatically with connected gimbal
2. ZR10_calibration.py          for camera calibration
3. siyi_set_angle.py            for setting gimbal camera pitch, yaw angle without touching it (change target_yaw, target_pitch)
